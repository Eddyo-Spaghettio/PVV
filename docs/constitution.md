# Proyecto Vidas Valiosas Constitution AY 2025-26

## Article I: Proyecto Vidas Valiosas

The organization shall be named Proyecto Vidas Valiosas (PVV).

## Article II: Purpose

The purpose of Proyecto Vidas Valiosas is to connect local non-profits and community organizations serving low-income communities with free, student-provided consulting services, providing technical, financial, and marketing support to support their operations and long-term success.

## Article III: Activities and Scope

Proyecto Vidas Valiosas will engage in a variety of activities related to local non-profits and community organizations, such as:

- Providing consulting services for marketing, fundraising, and digital presence for local non-profit organizations
- Develop long-term partnerships with local non-profits and community-based organizations (CBOs) to strengthen programs that support disadvantaged and low-income people/communities.
- Distribute information to Stanford students about local nonprofits to increase community engagement and event turnout.
- Organize volunteering events that gather Stanford students to serve local nonprofits directly, with transportation provided.

We also expect to:

- Collaborate with other organizations to host events and workshops that give members hands-on opportunities to explore consulting
- Host community building events to promote student engagement with nonprofit organizations.
- Facilitate speaking events with experts and those with lived experience in relation to financing nonprofits
- Conducting surveys and hosting focus groups with nonprofits to identify their needs and inform programming decisions.

Our service to nonprofits will mainly include, but is not limited to:

- Providing financial assistance:
  - Assisting nonprofits in the process of writing grants
- Providing marketing assistance in the field of advertising:
  - Social media management: creating and maintaining an online presence
  - Creating and distributing flyers promoting the nonprofit organization
  - Promoting upcoming events to Stanford students through the Haas Center for Public  Service
- Providing technical assistance through technical consulting and assistance in the realm of:
  - Creating websites, surveys, forms, and other online documents.
  - Creating pipelines for data collection and organization.
  - Technical advice.
  - Any other act of technical support related to increasing the efficiency of operations, data collection, user interaction, donations, and management.
- Assist non-profit clients with navigating benefits and connection to available resources.

## Article IV: Membership Requirements

Membership is open to all interested Stanford undergraduate and graduate students. Students on a leave of absence may not be active members, plan University-sponsored events, or hold leadership positions. To be considered a member, a student must have attended at least one meeting during their most recent quarter on campus and must be part of the group’s CardinalEngage portal.

Faculty, staff, post-doctoral scholars (postdocs), alumni, and other non-student community members may participate in events but cannot serve as leaders, members, or engage in group decision-making in any way. They also cannot represent the group or the university.

All students are welcome to participate in events and be members or leaders, regardless of gender.

## Article V: Leadership Structure and Officers

### Officers

The following roles oversee projects, community partnerships, and events, as well as make organizational decisions and elect leadership: All of the following roles are considered part of “operations” and will be referred to as such.

- PVV President: Responsible for overall management and oversight of PVV activities. Leads weekly general member meetings AND weekly operations meetings. Sets and evaluates performance of goals each quarter. Represents and publicizes club and Stanford facing events & opportunities. Upkeep partnership relations.
- PVV Vice President: Assists president with duties such as upkeep with partnerships, running meetings, and publicity. Oversees Community Consulting Chairs (CCCs) and assists with workflow and planning events. Eligible for election after 1 quarter or involvement.
- PVV Treasurer: Manages the clubs budget and keeps records of expenditures, reimbursements, and donations. Responsible for ensuring that relevant operational grants (Haas Transportation Grants, Haas Center Event Co-Sponsorship, etc.) are applied for and received. Serves as the primary point of contact for approving and managing club-related expenses.
- PVV Community Outreach Organizers (2): Responsible for organizing events related to PVV’s purpose such as consulting workshops, networking events,and volunteering opportunities. Identifies and connects with local affordability driven nonprofits. Spark partnerships with companies and nonprofits and other student leaders on Stanford campus to provide members with multiple opportunities and pathways.
- PVV Consulting Category Chairs (6): Responsible for directing student consulting efforts within their respective category (technical, financial, or marketing). Ensures that category-specific projects are progressing, delegated, and/or complete. Serves as a leader for students within each consulting specialization, overseeing student projects and collaborating with the recipient community organization to ensure their needs are being addressed.

### Elections

An election with all voting members must be held for President, Vice President, and Treasurer positions by April 15th. Other positions may be elected in the same manner or selected by a committee of at least five people from a variety of years.

Officer and Member Removal: If a member has concerns that an officer or member is not fulfilling their duties, they should bring their concern to the President (or Vice President, if the President is the person in question). The President should then notify the person in question about the concern and give them at least a week to attempt to resolve it. If the issue remains unresolved, the President should call a meeting. The person in question should be given an option to defend themselves in person at the meeting or provide a written statement. During the meeting, the person in question may be removed by a majority vote, with the person in question excluded from voting. If the group votes to remove an officer, another election will be held as soon as possible to fill the position.

Transition: From the time the new officers are elected to the end of spring quarter, the new officers should shadow and assist the outgoing officers to be prepared to take on their new roles. Any important documentation or records, including this constitution, should be shared with incoming officers. Officers will officially start and end their terms at the end of the spring quarter. This transition period should include leadership workshops, important contacts, and updating CardinalEngage. Each project team, and the board, should maintain a handbook with their standard operating procedures.

## Article VI: Decision Making Process

Decisions within the organization will be made through a consensus-building process. All officers and active members are encouraged to participate in discussions and share their opinions. For significant decisions that require a vote, a majority vote of the attending active members during a scheduled meeting will be required. The President, or any other officer, will not have the authority to make unilateral decisions.

If the organization is affiliated with external organizations, the relationship will be maintained while ensuring each party’s autonomy. The group will follow guidelines and policies set by the external organization in matters that are exclusively led by said organization, but will operate independently in all decision-making and activities not actively under the jurisdiction of the external group.

If the organization has an advisor, their role will be to provide guidance and support to the group without voting or making decisions on behalf of the group. The group should have the autonomy to choose their advisor.

## Article VII: Dissolution of Organization

In the event that the organization is dissolved, any remaining funds and equipment will be given to a related student group or a university department or center that the group works closely with (e.g., Haas Center for Public Service, Stanford Housing Equity Project, Stanford Consulting).

## Article VIII: Procedure to Amend Constitution

Amendments to this constitution can be proposed by any active member or officer. Proposed amendments should be discussed by all active members or officers and require a two-thirds majority vote of the attending active members during a scheduled meeting to pass. Once drafted and approved by members, final approval from OSE is required.
