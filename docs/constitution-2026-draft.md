# Proyecto Vidas Valiosas - Constitution (Revised Draft, AY 2026–27)

*Submitted for member review. Requires two-thirds vote + OSE approval to adopt.*

---

## Article I: Name

The organization shall be named **Proyecto Vidas Valiosas** ("PVV").

---

## Article II: Purpose

PVV connects Stanford students with community-based organizations serving low-income communities in the Bay Area. We provide free consulting in marketing, finance, and technology - and we help those organizations reach younger volunteers and donors through a standardized program called the **Bridge Kit**.

Our purpose is twofold:

1. **Support nonprofits** that lack the staff, budget, or digital capacity to grow their volunteer base and fundraising - especially among people under 25.
2. **Develop students** through hands-on consulting experience, ethical community engagement, and leadership in public service.

We prioritize long-term partnerships with organizations in East Palo Alto, Redwood City, and surrounding communities where affordability and access to basic services are ongoing challenges.

---

## Article III: What We Do

### Core Program: Bridge Kit

Every partner organization receives a **Bridge Kit**, a free package including:

- A mobile-friendly landing page for volunteer sign-up, donations, and contact
- A print-ready QR flyer for community distribution
- A 90-day social media content calendar with pre-written posts
- Promotion through Stanford channels (Haas Center listserv, CardinalEngage, campus events)
- An **Assigned Liaison** from PVV who recruits Stanford students and helps with digital outreach for one quarter

Bridge Kit is PVV's primary deliverable. The Assigned Liaison handles execution so clients are not adding a new job. Additional consulting (websites, grant writing, budgets, data systems) is offered based on client need and team capacity.

### Consulting Services

Beyond Bridge Kit, PVV teams may assist partners with:

**Marketing:** Social media management, flyer design, event promotion, messaging strategy

**Financial:** Budget creation, grant research and application support, funding navigation, case management assistance

**Technical:** Website creation, online forms and surveys, data collection systems, operational tech advice

**Resource navigation:** Connecting clients with benefits, referrals, and community resources

### Campus Activities

- Recruiting and training student consultants
- Organizing direct service and volunteering events (with transportation support from the Haas Center)
- Hosting workshops, speaker events, and community-building activities
- Collaborating with other Stanford service organizations
- Conducting needs assessments with partner organizations to improve our programming

## Article IV: Membership

### Eligibility

Membership is open to all Stanford undergraduate and graduate students. Students on leave of absence may not be active members, plan University-sponsored events, or hold leadership positions.

### Active Membership

A student is an active member if they:

1. Have attended at least one meeting during their most recent quarter on campus, AND
2. Are registered on the organization's CardinalEngage portal

### Non-Student Participation

Faculty, staff, postdocs, alumni, and community members may attend events but cannot be members, hold leadership positions, vote, or represent the organization.

All students are welcome regardless of gender, background, or experience level.

## Article V: Leadership

### Officers

| Role | Responsibilities |
|------|-----------------|
| **President** | Overall management. Leads general and operations meetings. Sets quarterly goals. Primary external representative. Maintains partner relationships. |
| **Vice President** | Supports President. Runs meetings when President is unavailable. Oversees consulting project workflow and Consulting Category Chairs. |
| **Treasurer** | Manages budget, reimbursements, and records. Applies for Haas transportation grants, OSE funding, and other operational grants. Approves club expenses. |
| **Outreach Organizer (×2)** | Identifies and connects with potential partner orgs. Organizes campus events and volunteering opportunities. Manages Stanford promotion channels. Coordinates Assigned Liaisons. |
| **Consulting Category Chair - Marketing** | Leads marketing consulting projects and Bridge Kit marketing components. Trains marketing team members. |
| **Consulting Category Chair - Financial** | Leads financial consulting projects. Trains financial team members. |
| **Consulting Category Chair - Technical** | Leads technical consulting projects and Bridge Kit Reach Page builds. Trains technical team members. |

All officers are part of **Operations** and attend weekly operations meetings in addition to general meetings.

### Assigned Liaison (Project Role, Not Officer)

Each quarter, Outreach Organizers name one PVV member as **Assigned Liaison** per active client. Liaisons serve as the primary student contact for that organization: recruiting peers, posting approved content, and attending at least one client event. The client approves content once at kickoff; the liaison owns ongoing execution. This is a defined project role with documented handoff, not an elected position.

### Elections - President, Vice President, and Treasurer are elected by active members by **April 15** each year. - Other positions may be elected or appointed by a committee of at least five members representing multiple class years. - Officers serve from end of Spring Quarter through the following Spring Quarter.

### Removal

If a member believes an officer is not fulfilling their duties, they should raise the concern with the President (or VP if the concern is about the President). The officer in question receives at least one week to address the issue. If unresolved, the President calls a meeting where the officer may respond in person or in writing. Removal requires a majority vote of attending active members, excluding the officer in question. Vacated officer positions are filled by election as soon as practicable.

### Transition

From election through end of Spring Quarter, incoming officers shadow outgoing officers. All documentation - including this constitution, client files, contact lists, and standard operating procedures - is transferred to incoming leadership. Each consulting track and the operations board maintains a handbook updated each quarter.

---

## Article VI: Decision Making

PVV uses consensus-building for most decisions. All officers and active members are encouraged to participate.

Significant decisions (new partnerships, budget allocations over $100, constitutional amendments, officer removal) require a **majority vote** of attending active members at a scheduled meeting.

The President does not have unilateral decision authority.

When affiliated with external organizations (Haas Center, OSE), PVV maintains autonomy in internal decisions while following external guidelines for funded activities.

If the organization has an advisor, they provide guidance without voting or making decisions on PVV's behalf. PVV selects its own advisor.

---

## Article VII: Standards of Practice

All PVV members working with community partners agree to:

1. Complete the Haas Center's Principles of Ethical and Effective Service training
2. Obtain client approval before publishing any materials on their behalf
3. Protect client confidentiality - no sharing client data or stories without permission
4. Document all deliverables and handoff procedures in the shared PVV Drive
5. Be honest about what students can and cannot deliver
6. Follow Stanford's Working with Minors policies when applicable

---

## Article VIII: Dissolution

If PVV is dissolved, remaining funds and equipment go to a related student group or university department (Haas Center for Public Service, Stanford Housing Equity Project, or similar).

---

## Article IX: Amendments

Any active member or officer may propose amendments. Amendments require discussion at a scheduled meeting and approval by **two-thirds** of attending active members. Final approval from OSE is required.
