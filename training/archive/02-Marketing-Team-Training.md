## Module 0: Welcome to the Hype Squad
**XP: 50 · Time: 30 min**

### What PVV Marketing Consulting Actually Is

Per our constitution, we help nonprofits with: - Social media management & online presence - Flyers and promotional materials - Promoting events to Stanford students (via **Haas Center** channels when appropriate) - Distributing info about local nonprofits to increase engagement

### What We Are NOT - A ad agency that guarantees virality - Ghostwriters who post without client approval - Meme Lords who sacrifice dignity for clicks (usually)

### The Ethical Marketing Oath (Read Aloud, Dramatically)

1. **Truth first** - No fake urgency, no invented impact numbers  
2. **Client voice** - They approve everything before it goes live  
3. **Dignity** - We serve low-income communities; never poverty-porn storytelling  
4. **Permission** - Photos need releases; stories need consent  
5. **Accessibility** - If it's not readable, it's not done  

### Marketing vs Fundraising vs Advocacy

| Activity | Goal | Example |
|----------|------|---------|
| **Marketing** | Awareness + action | "Come to block party!" |
| **Fundraising** | Money | "Donate $25 for backpacks" |
| **Advocacy** | Policy change | "Email your rep about rent caps" |

Know which mode you're in. NRC's block party is **marketing + light fundraising**.

### 🎯 Deliverable
Write NRC's **one-sentence mission** and **one-sentence event pitch**. Test on a roommate: do they know what/when/where in 10 seconds?

## Module 1: Know Thy Audience (Persona Power)
**XP: 100 · Time: 75 min**

### Story Hook: Ocean's Eleven, but the Heist Is Attention

Every campaign targets **specific people**, not "everyone." Even the Avengers don't recruit randomly - they pick specialists.

### Build 2–3 Audience Personas

A **persona** = fictional profile of a real audience segment.

**Template:**

```
NAME: Volunteer Vanessa
AGE: 21 · Stanford sophomore
GOALS: Meaningful community service, resume-worthy experience, free food events
PAIN POINTS: Doesn't have a car; skeptical of "voluntourism"
WHERE SHE SCROLLS: Instagram, GroupMe, Haas newsletter
WHAT MAKES HER ACT: Friend invite + clear impact + easy signup link
MESSAGE THAT WORKS: "No car? We’ve got rides. No experience? We’ve got training. Just show up."
CTA: Link to signup form
```

### Hands-On Lab: Create 3 Personas for NRC Block Party

Required personas:
1. **Stanford student volunteer** (PVV's specialty!)
2. **Local parent** (tutoring program target)
3. **Small business sponsor** (optional third)

For each, fill in: - Demographics (age range, location) - Motivations - Barriers - Channels - Message hook - Call to action (CTA)

### Audience → Channel Quick Map

| Audience | Primary channels | Content style |
|----------|------------------|---------------|
| Stanford students | Instagram, GroupMe, Haas promo, flyers at White Plaza | Casual, time/location bold, ride info |
| Local families | Facebook, WhatsApp groups, school flyers, community centers | Bilingual if possible, warm, kid-focused |
| Donors/sponsors | Email, LinkedIn | Impact stats + naming opportunities |

### 🎯 Deliverable
Three persona cards (1 page each). Circle your **primary** audience for the block party campaign.

## Module 2: Message Craft & Elevator Pitches
**XP: 100 · Time: 75 min**

### Story Hook: Shakespeare Wrote Tweets (If He Had To)

Good messaging is **simple, repeated, and true**.

### The Message Stack

```
MISSION (evergreen) → CAMPAIGN PROMISE (this event) → PROOF (why believe us) → CTA (what to do)
```

**NRC Example:**

| Layer | Copy |
|-------|------|
| Mission | Neighborhood Roots connects families to free tutoring and community resources. |
| Campaign promise | Block Party 2026: food, music, and free school supply kits while they last. |
| Proof | Last year, 300 neighbors attended and 80 kids enrolled in tutoring. |
| CTA | RSVP at nrc-bay.org/blockparty (link in bio) |

### Elevator Pitches (3 Lengths)

**10 seconds:**  
*"Neighborhood Roots is throwing a free block party April 18 - tacos, music, and school supply kits. RSVP in our bio!"*

**30 seconds:**  
Add: who you serve, one impact stat, volunteer ask

**60 seconds:**  
Add: story of one family helped, sponsorship mention, urgency ("first 100 families get supply kits")

### The CAPtain Clickbait CAP Framework - **C**oncise - Cut 30% of your first draft - **A**ction-oriented - Verb-first CTAs ("RSVP," "Volunteer," "Donate") - **P**roof - One stat or quote

### Words to Avoid / Words to Use

| 🚫 Avoid | ✅ Prefer |
|----------|-----------|
| "Synergistic community stakeholders" | "Neighbors like you" |
| "Underserved populations" (in external copy) | "Families in our community" |
| "Help us help them" | "Join us" |
| Vague "support our mission" | "Sign up to tutor 1 hr/week" |

### Hands-On Lab

Write for NRC Block Party: - [ ] 3 elevator pitches (10/30/60 sec - word counts ~25/75/150) - [ ] 5 headline options for a poster - [ ] 3 CTAs for different audiences

**Example headlines:**
1. *"Free Block Party · Free School Supplies · April 18"*
2. *"Tacos. Music. Community. April 18."*
3. *"Rooted Together - Redwood City's Block Party Returns"*

### 🎯 Deliverable
Message stack doc + favorite headline (circle one).

## Module 3: Channel Strategy - Pick 2, Not 12
**XP: 100 · Time: 60 min**

### Story Hook: Thanos Had the Right Idea About Focus

You cannot post expertly on TikTok, LinkedIn, Twitter, Instagram, Facebook, BeReal, carrier pigeon, **and** finish midterms.

**Rule:** Pick **2 primary channels** + **1 backup**.

### Channel Selection Matrix

Score 1–5 for NRC Block Party:

| Channel | Audience fit | Team capacity | Cost | **Total** |
|---------|--------------|---------------|------|-----------|
| Instagram | | | Free | |
| Facebook events | | | Free | |
| Physical flyers | | | $ print | |
| Haas Center promo | | | Free | |
| Email newsletter | | | Free | |

**Recommended for NRC + PVV learning:** - **Primary:** Instagram + physical flyers - **Stanford-specific add:** Haas Center promotional co-sponsorship request (see Module 5)

### Content Types by Channel

| Channel | Best content | Posting frequency (event campaign) |
|---------|--------------|-----------------------------------|
| Instagram | Reels, carousel, Stories countdown | 3x/week ramping up |
| Facebook | Event page, community groups | 2x/week + event page |
| Flyers | Bold headline, QR code, date/place | Print once, repost photo digitally |
| Email | Detailed info, volunteer shifts | 2 sends: 3 weeks out + 3 days out |

### The Repurpose Chain ♻️

One story → many formats:

```
Maria (tutoring student) success story
  → 60-sec interview clip (Reel)
  → Quote graphic (Instagram)
  → Pull quote on flyer
  → Email spotlight paragraph
```

Work smart. Captain Clickbait cries happy tears when you repurpose.

### 🎯 Deliverable
Channel plan: 2 primary + 1 backup, with rationale (1 paragraph).

## Module 4: Design Lab - Sunrise Block Party Poster
**XP: 150 · Time: 2 hours · GET CREATIVE**

### Story Hook: You're the Pixar Poster Department Now

This is the fun one. **Actually make a poster.**

### Poster Requirements Checklist - [ ] Event name: **Sunrise Community Block Party 2026** - [ ] Date, time, location (large readable text) - [ ] NRC logo placeholder (rectangle + "LOGO" is fine) - [ ] One hero image or illustration (stock photo OK for training) - [ ] QR code (link to Google Form RSVP for training) - [ ] CTA: "RSVP Today" or "Free Entry" - [ ] Contact / website - [ ] **Accessibility:** 18pt+ body text, high contrast

### Design Specs

| Format | Size | Use |
|--------|------|-----|
| Print flyer | 8.5×11" or 11×17" | Community boards, churches, shops |
| Instagram post | 1080×1080 px | Feed |
| Instagram story | 1080×1920 px | Countdown stickers |

### Canva Lab Steps

1. Open Canva → search "community event flyer"
2. Pick template with **big date typography**
3. Replace copy with NRC content
4. Colors: suggest warm sunrise palette (#FF6B35 orange, #004E89 blue, #F7C59F cream)
5. Add QR code (Canva element → link to your practice form)
6. Export PDF (print) + PNG (social)

### Design Principles (Pixel Patel's Rules)

1. **Hierarchy:** Date should be readable from 6 feet away on a bulletin board
2. **One focal point:** Don't compete with yourself
3. **White space:** Crowded flyers = ignored flyers
4. **Mobile test:** Shrink to phone size - is text still readable?

### Example Poster Copy Block

```
SUNRISE COMMUNITY BLOCK PARTY
Rooted Together, Rising Together

📅 Saturday, April 18, 2026 · 11am–4pm
📍 Cesar Chavez Plaza, Redwood City

FREE tacos · Live music · Kids crafts · Resource fair
First 100 families receive FREE school supply kits

[QR CODE]
RSVP: nrc-bay.org/blockparty

Neighborhood Roots Collective · nrc-bay.org · @NeighborhoodRootsRC
```

### Optional Creative Challenges 🏆 - **Bilingual version** (English/Spanish) - **"What if Pixar designed it?"** rebrand (fun exercise) - **Minimalist version** with only 20 words total

## Module 6: Measure What Matters
**XP: 100 · Time: 60 min**

### Story Hook: Sherlock Holmes, but for Analytics

Vanity metrics (likes) aren't mission metrics (volunteers signed up).

### Pick Metrics That Match Goals

| NRC Goal | Metric | Tool |
|----------|--------|------|
| 50 new volunteers | Form submissions | Google Forms |
| 100 tutoring signups | Program intake forms | Google Sheets |
| $5,000 raised | Donation total | Givebutter / PayPal |
| Awareness | RSVP count, flyer scans (QR) | Bitly click stats |

### SMART Marketing Goals

**Bad:** "Go viral"  
**Good:** "Achieve 200 RSVP form submissions by April 17 via Instagram (60%) and flyers (40%)"

### Simple Tracking Sheet

| Metric | Target | Week 1 | Week 2 | Week 3 | Event day | Notes |
|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|-----------|-------|
| RSVPs | 200 | | | | | |
| IG reach | 5,000 | | | | | |
| Volunteers signed | 50 | | | | | |

### After-Action Report (Template)

1. What we planned vs what happened  
2. Top performing channel  
3. One thing to repeat  
4. One thing to kill  
5. Client quote / story collected  

### 🎯 Deliverable
Measurement plan + blank after-action report template for NRC.
