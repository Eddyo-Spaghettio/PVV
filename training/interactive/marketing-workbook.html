Module 1: Strategy Before Posting

Most small nonprofits fail at marketing not because they're bad at it, but because they try to do everything at once. One Instagram post won't fix a visibility problem. A clear plan might.

The rule One goal. One audience. One or two channels. Master those before adding anything else.

Module 2: Building Bridge Kit Marketing Assets

Bridge Kit is PVV's core deliverable. The marketing team owns three of its five components: the QR flyer, the 90-day content calendar, and the Stanford Channel Pack.

The QR Flyer

A stranger at a laundromat or church bulletin board should understand in 3 seconds: what's happening, when, where, and what to do. The QR code goes to the Reach Page (technical team builds that).

Must include: event/org name (biggest text), date + time, location, one impact line, QR code, logo.

Tools: Canva (free), Adobe Express, or Google Slides. Use the client's colors. Print at Haas copiers or FedEx.

Exercise: Write flyer copy

The 90-Day Content Calendar

You're not inventing posts from scratch every week. You're filling in a template. Open templates/bridge-kit/90-day-calendar-template.csv in Google Sheets - it has 24 pre-planned posts. Customize the bracketed fields for your client.

Cadence that works for small orgs 2–3 posts per week beats daily mediocre posts. Consistency over volume.

Caption formula

Hook - question, stat, or bold statement
Story - 2–3 sentences about why this matters
Details - date, time, location, link
CTA - one clear action ("Link in bio to sign up")

Module 3: Launch, Promote, Measure

Posting is half the job. The other half is making sure the right people see it, show up, and come back.

Launch sequence

WhenAction
2 weeks before eventTeaser post + service4all email
1 week beforeFlyer goes up (QR codes in community + Haas)
3 days beforeCountdown post + Assigned Liaison shares on personal account
Day ofStory posts, live updates, photos
Week afterThank-you post with numbers + "see you next time"

Assigned Liaison role

Every Bridge Kit includes one PVV member as the Assigned Liaison for the quarter. As a marketing team member, you might be that liaison. Your job: post approved content, recruit Stanford peers, share on your personal account, attend one client event. The client approves copy once at kickoff; you handle execution so they are not adding a new job. Document everything in the handoff template for the next liaison.
